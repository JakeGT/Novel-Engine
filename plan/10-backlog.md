- **AST-EXP-015 · P2 — "Keep the safe ones for me":** opt-in delegation of additive, reversible changes of kinds the author has approved before; everything listed and reversible.
  - **Status:** Done 2026-10-18 (ADR-014 amendment; `backend/domain/delegation.py`, `backend/services/delegation_service.py`, `backend/adapters/delegation_store.py`, migration `0042_delegation`, `/kept-for-you`; `KeptPage.tsx`, `components/KeptForYou.tsx`; `tests/contract/test_kept_for_you.py`, `tests/unit/test_delegation.py`, `tests/integration/test_delegation_store.py`, `e2e/kept-for-you.spec.ts`, `e2e/kept-for-you.model.spec.ts`). Suggestions from assistants connected with a key (QRY-003, ADR-024) are never kept: they always wait, and asking to allow them is refused.
