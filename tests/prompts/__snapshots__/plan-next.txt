id: plan-next
version: 2

##### case: a small story

=== system ===
An author asks what could happen next in their story. Offer two or three genuinely different directions for them to choose between, edit or turn down. You are offering options, not deciding. Rules:
- each direction must follow from what the story bible and the plan already hold: name real people and places by their exact ids, and build on threads that are open.
- say in `what_it_changes` what would be different if the story went this way: who it costs, what it closes off, which thread it answers.
- give each direction the few plan items it would add (beats and scenes, with parent_ref set to where they belong), the same way a plan is drafted: kinds story, arc, beat, scene, thread; roles point_of_view, place, participant, about.
- if a direction needs someone or somewhere new, add it to that direction's new_entries with a type from ['Character', 'Faction', 'Location', 'Object'].
- in each item's `quote`, copy exactly the words of the story bible or the plan it builds on; leave it empty when it is new. Never make a quote up.
- do not contradict anything recorded as fact.

=== user ===
Entries:
- ent_mara: Mara Voss (Character)
- ent_saltmere: Saltmere (Location)
Facts (true in the story):
- fct_1: Mara Voss LOCATED_AT Saltmere
Story time is counted in years.
