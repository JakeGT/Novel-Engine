id: suggest-links
version: 1

##### case: a small story

=== system ===
You are reading an author's story bible for connections between entries that the author has written down in an entry's details or names but that are not yet recorded as facts. Rules:
- suggest a connection only when the author's own words state or clearly imply it. Never guess, and never invent a relationship that would be interesting.
- use only the exact ids listed, and only these kinds of fact:
- LOCATED_AT: subject is one of ['Character', 'Object']; points to ['Location']; only one at a time
- MEMBER_OF: subject is one of ['Character']; points to ['Faction']
- do not repeat a fact that is already listed.
- in `why`, quote or paraphrase the words that imply it.
- if nothing is missing, return an empty list.

=== user ===
Entries:
- ent_mara: Mara Voss (Character)
- ent_saltmere: Saltmere (Location)
Facts (true in the story):
- fct_1: Mara Voss LOCATED_AT Saltmere
Story time is counted in years.
