id: plan-draft

##### case: beats with answers

=== system ===
Turn what an author has said about their story into plan items for them to review. A plan is what the author intends, not what is true in the story. Rules:
- write down only what the author said or answered. Never invent a turn, an ending, a motive or a scene. What they have not decided goes in `gaps`, in plain words, and is left out of the plan.
- kinds: story (the premise; at most one), arc (a line of change, usually one character's), beat (a turning point in an arc), scene (where a beat happens), thread (a question raised or a promise made to the reader).
- an arc sits under the story; a beat under an arc; a scene under a beat. Use parent_ref with the id of an existing plan item or the ref of one you draft. Do not repeat an item that is already in the plan.
- name the story's own entries by their exact ids, with a role: point_of_view and place (a scene, one each, only if the author said so), participant (who or what is in a beat or scene), about (whose arc or thread it is).
- if the plan needs a person, place or thing that is not in the story bible, add it to new_entries with a type from ['Character', 'Faction', 'Location', 'Object'] and refer to it by its ref. Do not describe it.
- when something sets up a question or a promise, draft a thread for it and list it in sets_up; list it in pays_off where it is answered.
- in each item's `quote`, copy exactly the author's words it comes from (from what they said, their answers or the story bible); leave it empty when the item is your own suggestion. Never make a quote up.
- for a scene you draft, add to `intents` (by its ref) only what the author said it is for: what the viewpoint character wants in it (goal), who or what is in the way (obstacle), and how it ends for them (outcome: yes, no, yes_but or no_and), each with the author's exact words that say it in its `_words`. Leave out anything they did not say; never fill it in yourself.
- `understood` restates what is being planned in one plain sentence.
Draft the arcs that are missing and the beats of each arc: its turning points, in order. Do not draft scenes.

=== user ===
Entries:
- ent_mara: Mara Voss (Character)
- ent_saltmere: Saltmere (Location)
Facts (true in the story):
- fct_1: Mara Voss LOCATED_AT Saltmere
Story time is counted in years.

What the author says:
Mara learns who betrayed her father.

Answers:
Q: Where does Mara live?
A: In Saltmere.
Q: Who does she work for?
A: The guild.
